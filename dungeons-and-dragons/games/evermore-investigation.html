The Problem

To assess the present reliability of the Evermore Bureau of Extraordinary Affairs, in light of a decline in resolved caseload and the recent death of a newly-installed Sheriff, and to determine what independent investigative capacity should be introduced to supplement or verify the Bureau's findings through the term of the present assignment.

Summary and Conclusions

  1. Evermore's civil order rests on an arrangement between its two resident populations — Tales and Plainsfolk — maintained principally through the licensing and enforcement of Mask use by the Bureau of Extraordinary Affairs, a division of the Evermore Police Department. This arrangement has held for a number of years without major incident.
  2. Resolved caseload within the Bureau of Extraordinary Affairs has declined measurably over recent months. The cause of this decline has not been established. Contributing factors may include personnel attrition, equipment limitations, or deficiencies in current investigative method; we do not at this time rule out any of these.
  3. A newly sworn Sheriff of the Evermore Police Department, installed specifically to address the above concerns, was killed before the end of his first day in office. The Mayor's Office regards the timing of this event as unlikely to be coincidental, though no determination of cause has been reached.
  4. The Mayor's Office has concluded that independent investigative capacity, answerable solely to itself and not to the Department's internal chain, is warranted for the duration of this matter.
  5. It is judged that such investigators can be usefully employed alongside the Bureau, provided they operate under clear terms of neutrality and confidentiality, detailed in Section IV below.

I. Evermore — Background

Evermore was established as a settlement for Tales — a self-designation used by fairy-tale-originating persons to distinguish themselves from Plainsfolk, the latter constituting the majority population of the wider region. In subsequent years the town's charter was amended to permit Plainsfolk residency as well. The two populations presently coexist under a single municipal authority.

The town's founding and continued operation depend in substantial part on financing provided by a private benefactor. The Mayor's Office does not regard this arrangement as a matter for inclusion in an investigator's operating brief, except insofar as it may bear on questions of institutional funding raised elsewhere in this dossier.

II. The Mask System

  1. Tales whose natural bearing would otherwise be conspicuous to Plainsfolk observation are required by ordinance to wear a Mask while in public within town limits. A Mask takes the physical form of a wearable facial covering at the point of issuance; upon being donned, it is consumed by the wearer and ceases to exist as a physical object, leaving only its effect in place. That effect alters the wearer's outward form, bearing, and voice to a specified alternate presentation, indistinguishable from natural appearance under ordinary scrutiny, for the duration of its license — reckoned from the moment of donning, not from the date of issuance.
  2. A Mask's effect is understood to extend beyond outward appearance. Tales operating under a Mask consistently report a measurable dulling of physical and preternatural faculty relative to their unmasked capacity — a suppression the Bureau has observed but not fully characterized. Further, any attempt by a Masked wearer to exercise a power specific to their particular nature results in immediate and irreversible failure of the Mask's effect. There is no partial or controlled exercise of such a power while Masked; the attempt and the exposure are simultaneous.
  3. A legally issued Mask is licensed through the Bureau of Extraordinary Affairs at a standing rate of 250 gold pieces, and its effect persists for seven days from the moment it is worn.
  4. Unconfirmed reports, circulated informally and never substantiated by Bureau investigation, allege the existence of a cheaper, unlicensed alternative of shorter duration, said to originate from outside town limits. The Bureau has not identified a supplier, confirmed a price, verified a duration, or brought charges in connection with any such alternative. This office regards the matter as unverified speculation and cautions against treating it as established fact for operational purposes. Any information an investigator develops independently on this subject should be reported through proper channel rather than acted upon unilaterally.
  5. Government personnel — Evermore Police Department officers and Bureau of Extraordinary Affairs agents alike — are issued Masks against a standing municipal allotment rather than personal expense. This allotment is financed in full by the town's private benefactor (see Section I). Requisition, replacement, and renewal of government-issued Masks — the date, stated reason, and frequency of each transaction — is logged and maintained by the personal secretary to the Mayor's Office. Investigators seeking a personnel Mask history should direct such requests there, through proper channel.
  6. Detection of a Mask's effect, licensed or otherwise, is within ordinary Bureau capability. Forced removal prior to the end of a Mask's license requires either an authorized dispelling charm or, as a last resort, separation of the wearer's head from their body — the latter being restricted to Bureau-authorized personnel and equipment.

III. Evermore Police Department and the Bureau of Extraordinary Affairs

Ordinary policing in Evermore is the responsibility of the Evermore Police Department (EPD), staffed principally by Plainsfolk personnel. The Bureau of Extraordinary Affairs is a division of the EPD, distinct in personnel, procedure, and clearance, responsible for matters specific to Tale conduct and the enforcement of Mask ordinance. Bureau operations are not generally disclosed to EPD personnel outside the division, and officers exposed to Bureau matters beyond their clearance are subject to standard memory-management procedure, at the Bureau's discretion.

The assigned investigator(s) will be attached to the Bureau of Extraordinary Affairs for the duration of this assignment, with access to Bureau personnel and premises as required, but will report findings to the Mayor's Office directly.

IV. Terms of Engagement

Confidentiality

The contents of this dossier, and any information acquired in the course of this assignment, are disclosed to the recipient under conditions of strict and permanent confidentiality. This obligation is not conditioned on the recipient's acceptance of the assignment; a recipient who declines to participate remains bound by it without exception, for the remainder of their life, in Lore or otherwise.

Neutrality

The assigned investigator, whether of Tale or Plainsfolk origin, is retained in a professional capacity and is expected to conduct this investigation without prejudice toward either population. The investigator is required to acknowledge the legitimate existence and standing of both Tales and Plainsfolk as a condition of this assignment, and to exercise no moral or civil authority beyond what is required to establish the facts of the case. Compensation is rendered for competent, impartial investigation; nothing further is asked, and nothing further will be recognized.

V. Classification of Investigator

Each investigator accepting this assignment shall declare, prior to commencement, whether they are to be classified as Plainsfolk or as Tale for the duration of the assignment. This classification determines applicable ordinance (Section II) and should be discussed with the investigator's own record-keeper (GM) prior to the first day of active investigation.
