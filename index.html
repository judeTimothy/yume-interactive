Pandapay UI/UX Case Study.

Featured Case

Redesigning Pandapay to reduce payment friction and improve user support.

Background

Participants45 Survey · 5 Interviews
RoleUX Research · UI/UX Design
FocusPayment Experience

This case study focused on the payment experience within local food delivery apps, with attention placed on Pandapay as Foodpanda’s in-app wallet. Through surveys and 1:1 interviews, the study found that users were less concerned with food review features than with payment friction, including double-charging, failed transactions, cash-in limitations, withdrawal restrictions, and difficulty getting timely support.

The research showed that frequent food delivery users needed a faster, more reliable, and more flexible payment experience. Users valued convenience, security, and control over their remaining balance, but existing wallet limitations and verification steps created frustration across the transaction journey.

Outcome

The proposed design solutions focused on two main opportunities: helping users maximize the use of Pandapay and helping users feel supported during payment-related issues. The concept introduced expanded wallet usability through QR-based transactions and fewer balance restrictions, alongside a persistent in-app support entry point for faster troubleshooting.

This project demonstrates a full UX process grounded in user research, problem validation, persona building, synthesis, and solution prototyping.
