Character Progression — Level Up Tracker

Maximum level is 6. Each level-up advances one Tier in an Ability of your choice — pick one feature from that Tier; the rest can't be taken again for that Tier. Ability Score Improvement is available at Tier II and Tier V. Shade a circle to mark your plan.

Evasion = Grace bonus + Archetype bonus + Armor modifier. Medium Armor caps the Grace-bonus portion at +2; Heavy Armor drops total Evasion to 0 regardless of bonus or archetype.
