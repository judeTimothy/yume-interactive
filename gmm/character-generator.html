Character Progression — Level Up Tracker

Maximum level is 6. Each level-up grants one point to spend on a Tier feature in any Ability — Tiers must be taken in order within an ability (Tier II requires Tier I already taken, and so on). Ability Score Improvement is available at Tier II and Tier V.

Evasion = Grace bonus + Archetype bonus + Armor modifier. Medium Armor caps the Grace-bonus portion at +2; Heavy Armor drops total Evasion to 0 regardless of bonus or archetype.
